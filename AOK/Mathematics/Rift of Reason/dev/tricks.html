Trick bank (#111)

Every trick round of the roaming legendaries, one random version each. An attempt draws 12: four of the creature's own tricks and eight shared tricks in its theme. At most one is a timed Quick! round; counting has no deadline. A wrong answer sends the creature running.

The answer is in green. "Why" is what a student sees after a wrong answer. Automatic checks (tools/test/tricks.test.mjs) make sure every round has one fair answer that follows from the screen. Press Reroll to see other random versions.

Teacher test notes

Every arena has a solver-checked winning path. The ghost trail shows this cast, not a whole solution. Steady follows its arrow and turns right at a wall; Turns turns right on every third step; Net hunter attacks an adjacent new net at the first step. Each cast gives it up to three steps. A hit breaks that net and uses the cast. Great Charm and lure each add one cast here.

Try a fresh arena without extra help, then the same seed with help. Does the loss make sense? Would you play again? Student success rate, 20–60-second pacing and fun still need playtesting. This prototype does not replace the campaign catch game yet.

# #129 expansion-3: the third small catalogue batch

Base: `c7e6ee8` (main, after #163 slice 1). Scope: release 8 of the 177 held catalogue concepts, using the #123 method and its "Continuation under #129" rules ([audit](../drafts/card-arena-2026-10-12/CONTRACT-AUDIT-123.md)). **169 concepts stay held.** The 18 story-only cards stay out of packs. No new Fate, Win, token or reaction rule is added.

All six Ways and generic are covered, with 1 Rare, 2 Uncommons and 5 Commons. Every card uses its existing selected portrait or its Way's spell motif, the shared play/hit/block/heal sounds and no voice lines. Nothing was added to the art request list, and no audio was generated.

**Engine additions.** Niklas needed a Last Word that is not about returning to the hand. The existing `lastWord` hook is skipped when the hand is full or Mercy already returns the card, which would silently skip his effect. A new `lastWordEffect` hook runs on every defeat (still off under Silence and Empty Set) and never moves the card itself. `recoverTop` is the top-of-deck twin of the existing `recover`. The discard pile is public, so the card on top is public information; previews and the AI do not read the hidden deck through it (tested).

**Contract clarifications** (in `review-129.mjs`; meaning unchanged): Susie skips a creature that already has Guard; Peter measures once on the board and never lowers his own attack; Niklas's Last Word also happens with a full hand or Mercy; Proof by Cases offers only a case that can happen. The student card text is shorter than the contract text.

**Card text (#163).** All four creature rules fit the 300 px framed face. Check the Premise, Focused Hit and Simple Cover print a short rule on the tactic plate. Proof by Cases is too long for the plate, so, like other long tactics since #163, its big face names the card ("Reason · rare") and the notes and reader carry the whole rule. `card-text.test.mjs` checks every released card, and `expansion-3.test.mjs` checks these eight directly.

## Tests

`tools/test/expansion-3.test.mjs` adds 13 tests. They cover:

- **Each hook:** Terry's one-turn boost and that it never targets himself; Susie's timed Guard through the opponent's turn and its end; Peter's copy, cap, expiry, no-question path, choice between two higher sources, aura-adjusted measure and never-lower rule; Niklas's exact deck order and log; both damage spells reading Guard, no keywords, silence, timed Guard and Shield; Simple Cover's expiry; both Proof by Cases cases with two and three friendly creatures.
- **Empty source and no target:** Terry or Susie alone, Susie with only Guard creatures, Peter with nothing higher, Niklas with no spell in the pile, damage spells with no enemy or only an Elusive one, Simple Cover with no friendly creature or only Guards, and Proof by Cases with only one case possible or none (not offered).
- **Loop and re-entry:**
  - Empty Set turns off all three new Entrances, which then ask for no target, and nothing fires late after the rule leaves and a turn passes. Silence and Empty Set also turn off Niklas's Last Word.
  - Rethink then replay is a fresh paid play: Peter's copy is cleared, then exactly one new copy.
  - Two Terrys on one friend give +2, and both end together. A second Susie cannot pick the creature the first one already guards. Proof by Cases twice gives +4 maximum health in two separate casts.
  - Two Niklas defeated together make two Last Words: the two oldest spells go on top, and no card is copied.
  - Niklas's Last Word still happens with a full hand and under Mercy (Mercy still returns him).
  - An AI self-play fuzz with the whole batch checks that every game ends, every card stays in one zone, every creature is played and the three damage spells are played.
- **Information:** previews and all three AI levels choose the same action when the hidden deck order changes, with Niklas about to fall.
- **Sources and saves:** one ordinary chapter-two catch per creature, all eight in the pack roster with their rarities, story exclusion, the four-copy cap, and creature copies plus normal and foil spell prints surviving both the plain save and a packed backup code.
- **Metadata and text:** runtime data matches the catalogue, handlers are real, portraits and motifs exist in the asset manifest, and each card's printed rule fits the #163 budget.

After the Standards review, Peter's rule, the Proof by Cases rule and its two creature prompts, and one Check the Premise log line were made shorter and plainer. The Spec review added a literal aura case for Peter and the plain-save round trip.

13/13 pass. One existing test changed: the mounted stall test reads the pre-unlock odds line, which now counts 17/4/2 kinds instead of 12/2/1. The odds percentages are unchanged.

No test failed from sampling noise this time, so no sample size or bar changed. The Hard-vs-Easy check from expansion-2 (80 games, 60% bar) passes as is.

**A real defect the existing fuzz found.** The first full run had one failure: `battle-arena-fuzz` ("2,000 random games") reported "offered p0t5 fizzled". In game 129, a Focused Hit defeated the rival's Niklas when their discard pile held no spell. His "finds no spell" note was emitted as a `fizzle` event, which the board treats as the player's own action failing. It now goes in the log as an ordinary ability line, and `expansion-3.test.mjs` asserts that no fizzle appears there. After the fix, all 2,000 fuzz games pass. The test itself was not changed.

**Full suite** (under the 12 GB memory cap): 1,494 tests, 1,493 pass, 1 skip, 0 fail (the baseline on `c7e6ee8` was 1,481 tests, 1,480 pass, 1 skip).

`validate-planning.mjs` now checks all four runtime batches and asserts that 169 are held. `validate-personas.mjs` checks that no held creature has a runtime definition. Both pass, and the regenerated catalogue, persona, roster and review files are byte-stable.

## Paired mono/mixed games and the mirror, now with partner Ways from the colour wheel

`node tools/sim-expansion-3.mjs 16` (and `--without-batch-hooks`) plays 16 seeds × 2 seats at Competent vs Competent. Each Way's mono deck meets **three** mixed decks: its own Way plus one partner Way. The partners come from the colour wheel (`Rift.data.wheel.order`: reason, emotion, language, perception, imagination, memory; each Way is Strong against the next two and Weak against the previous two):

- the **next** Way on the wheel, which the mono Way is Strong against;
- the **previous** Way, which the mono Way is Weak against;
- the **opposite** Way, which is neutral.

That gives 576 mono-vs-mixed games, plus 192 **mono mirror** games (the same deck on both sides, so any split there is seat, first-player and seed noise). That is 768 games each way. All decks are legal 20-card personal decks with the ten shared Axioms. The batch creature leads its Way's deck; Reason and Perception got spells only, so they keep their foundation bodies, and Reason carries both of its spells. Reports: `expansion-3-2026-10-23/mono-mixed-probe.json` and `mono-mixed-without-hooks.json`.

**Why three partners.** Expansion-2's probe took the "next" Way from the fixture list (reason, emotion, perception, language, …), not from the wheel. So its single partner was usually a Strong or Weak wheel edge, and the skews it showed could not be told apart from colour strength. A first run of this probe used next and previous from the same fixture list, and the Spec review caught the same flaw there. That run was replaced; the numbers below use the wheel.

**The control.** With hooks off, creatures keep their bodies, costs and keywords (Niklas keeps Guard) and lose only the new hook. Each spell keeps a plain base effect, so no control deck carries a dead card: Check the Premise always deals 1, Focused Hit always 2, Proof by Cases only its 4-damage case, and Simple Cover becomes a plain heal 2 (as #123 kept Small Patch).

**Noise.** Across the 12 mirror rows, seat 0's share runs from 0.38 to 0.66, up to ±0.16 around even. As in expansion-2, one 32-game share has a standard deviation of about 0.09, so the difference between two shares has about 0.125, and two standard deviations is about ±0.25.

**Reading the batch.** All 18 changes are inside that noise (−0.19 to +0.09). The three largest moves (−0.19) are Perception against Perception+Language, Language against Language+Emotion and Imagination against Imagination+Memory. Pooled over all 576 mono-vs-mixed games, the mono share is 0.54 with the hooks and 0.59 without, a tilt of −0.05 towards the mixed decks. That is about 1.7 standard deviations, so it is not clear evidence. It points the way #123 asked later batches to go: a mixed deck draws on two Ways' new cards, a mono deck on one. Sixteen seeds cannot show a batch effect for certain, and no numbers were tuned.

**Reading the fixtures (what the extra partners were for).**

- **The wheel explains the big skews.** Averaged over the six Ways, the mono share is 0.74 against its Strong edge, 0.27 against its Weak edge and 0.62 against the neutral one (0.79, 0.36 and 0.63 with hooks off). Expansion-2's "Perception mono is weak" was Perception against its Weak edge (Language), and its "Memory mono is strong" was Memory against its Strong edge (Reason). Neither was a sign of colour strength.
- **Colour or fixture strength, from the neutral row.** Most mono decks win about 0.56–0.63 against the opposite Way. That suggests a small general edge for a mono deck in these fixtures. Two stand out. The **Reason** mono fixture is strong (0.84, and 0.91 with hooks off). The **Memory** mono fixture is the weakest (0.44, and 0.38 with hooks off). Both predate this batch, since the hooks-off control agrees. Both are recorded here and not tuned. With 32 games per row, only Reason clearly passes the noise.

**Plays and bounds.** Every new card was played in the hooks-on run: Simple Cover 658, Niklas 486, Susie 462, Focused Hit 403, Check the Premise 348, Peter 334, Terry 283, Proof by Cases 153. Every action kept each card in one zone. No hooks-on game reached the turn limit; the longest took 176 actions. The only draws were 4 hooks-off Reason mirror games that hit the turn limit.

## Browser verification

- **Battle board.** On `dev/expansion-3.html`, I ran the production board with a memory-only save in headless Firefox over WebDriver BiDi, using real pointer input. Each of the 8 cards, plus a second Niklas sample in which your Focused Hit defeats the rival's Niklas, was committed at 1280×720 normal, at 1280×720 Calm (mouse) and at 390×844 Calm (touch taps). That is 27 commits, and all reached the expected state:
  - Check the Premise dealt 2 to the Guard Lobstorian; Focused Hit dealt 3 to the keyword-free Beastie.
  - Terry took Paper Dragon from 2 to 3 attack.
  - Susie and Simple Cover gave Paper Dragon Guard; Proof Pup (already Guard) was not offered.
  - Peter copied Khaby's 4 attack with no question.
  - Niklas landed with Guard. Defeated, his Last Word put the rival's Counterexample (their oldest discarded spell) on top of the rival's deck, and the log said so.
  - Proof by Cases showed the two case buttons. At 1280 the damage case and then the target buttons defeated Lobstorian; under Calm (both widths) the support case gave Paper Dragon and Proof Pup +2 maximum health with no second question.
- **Calm, width and errors.** Under Calm, no running animation used transform, translate, scale or rotate. The document width stayed 390 on the phone. The browser log had no errors or warnings.
- **Pack stall.** At the Campfire Clearing, real presses on the stall bought a five-card pack at 1280 and at 390 (touch). It held Niklas Landillo and Susie Dentshell with three earlier cards. The reveal played card by card, showing Niklas's full rule and "New copy · Folder 1/4".
- **Not verified in the browser:** a full chapter-two catch (spawn weights are tested), a physical touch screen, the OS reduced-motion setting and `file://`.

After the review text fixes I re-ran all 27 board commits. Every outcome was identical, with no errors and no transform motion under Calm. Screenshots and `browser-results.json` are beside this report. The bench and scratch driver hold no real progress.

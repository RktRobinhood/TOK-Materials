## Limits

Solver acceptance establishes internal consistency. Mounted fake-DOM checks establish specific controller behavior, not a full browser route. Static and source checks do not establish voice delivery, every random variant, mobile/touch coverage or learning outcomes. Classroom follow-up should still observe an uncoached learner making and explaining the next decision; completion and stars alone are not evidence of understanding.

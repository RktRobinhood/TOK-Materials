## Remaining problems

1. **midday-pie · resolve · Why · the correct option is wordy.** "He was at the sill, half asleep, and woke up full. Crumbs and fork too." It has 14 words, two sentences and a fragment, and it reads as a checklist, not a reason.
   → `Only he woke up full, with crumbs and a fork.`

2. **midday-pie · intro · Fox inner line · an idiom twist (my own round 1 wording, sorry).** "…Too good to be false?" only works if you know "too good to be true". Non-native readers will stall.
   → `Picture it: a cat burglar. Tiny mask. Tiny rope. I love it. I want it true.`
   (This keeps Fox's blind spot: the best story, not the likeliest.)

3. **witness-tent · outcome 1 · an unclear quote in a note.** 'Speedcheeta shows the zoomed-out clip. Just the trophy stand. "The rest is… private."' A skim reader can't tell what "the rest" is. It is also unvoiced dialogue inside a note. The beat this should pay off is the crowd finally seeing it.
   → note: `Speedcheeta shows the crowd the whole clip. Black and white. The crowd goes "Ohhh."`

4. **witness-tent · resolve · safety argument · the joke was lost when safety became a "care".** "…Corks. Yes. I don't love corks. Go on." This is the only flat Corvina line. Bring back the old joke and keep the acceptance:
   → `I've been hit by Tuesdays. Corks, though… Fine. Go on.`

5. **midday-pie · intro · Moth-kin blue reply ignores the glint.** The hook says "Something glints in the pocket", but Rawmsay's answer talks only about flour and pastry.
   → `Flour. Chefs wear flour. …And pastry. And a fork. Stop looking.`

6. **lucky-well · well spot · "I do the lighting!" (optional).** This fits the Ch1 ring-light look. After the Gate the ring light is smashed ("That was my whole FACE!"), so in the mask and clipboard looks the line doesn't quite fit. Keep it for `NUDGE_CH1`. For the later looks, use `Get that light off me! I'm in disguise!`

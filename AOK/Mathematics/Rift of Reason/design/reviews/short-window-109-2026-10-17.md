# Compact short-window table (#109), 17 October 2026

Teacher note (17 Oct): in windows under 620px tall (a laptop with toolbars), the battle scrolled and the hand started below the fold. Nothing was cropped, but the first view was poor.

## What changed

`css/arena.css` has a new block for **wide, short windows**: at least 851px wide and 500–620px tall. It replaces the scrolling layout there:

- The battle is clipped to the window (`overflow: clip`), with no 700px minimum height.
- The hand card width comes from the window height: `--hw: clamp(54px, 11.4cqh, 68px)`. The bottom row (`--hand-row`) is the hand card's height plus 12px, so the hand is always fully in view.
- Board cards take whatever height is left, up to 84px wide, and still fit the width-based limit used on bigger screens.
- The top row is 46px and the lane 108px (Fate track 54px).
- Deck backs scale with height (`clamp(40px, 9cqh, 56px)`). Your deck sits just above the hand row, and the shared axiom deck is centred on the lane.

## Builder decisions

- **The lower bound is 500px.** At 480px the board cards shrink to 48px and names stop being readable. Below 500px, and on narrow screens (850px or less), the old scrolling layout stays.
- **The draw glow and deck size from the 13 Oct note were already delivered** in #122 (c6dae43, 14 Oct): `draw-ready` follows engine legality, and owner backs are 70px at desktop size. This slice did not change them.

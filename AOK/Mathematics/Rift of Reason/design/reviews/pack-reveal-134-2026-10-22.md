## Builder calls

- **Folder count saved with the pack.** The Spec review showed that a count worked out from today's save drifts if the student catches or releases a copy before Done (a receipt waits across reloads). `Packs.draw` now records `copies` (the folder count right after that copy) inside the same purchase transaction. It is display only and changes no reward. Backup codes accept it (a whole number ≥ 1) or its absence; older receipts fall back to today's count.
- **Sounds.** The pack's `open` plays on the first press and the first card's sound 0.3 s later; then one sound per press. Skip is silent (it stops the pack's sounds); finishing the reveal plays the `jingle` once.
- **Escape** skips to the list (the shared dialog's Escape only knows Close/Cancel-style buttons). The list itself needs Done.
- **Axiom faces** use the Axiom's own art on the battle table's parchment glow, with light ink on the dark Axiom panel; spell faces use dark ink on their parchment panel.
- **Solid dialog.** The reveal and the list open over the stall, so both use an opaque panel; the stall's text no longer shows through.
- `Packs.describe` (only used by the old static list) is gone; the list now repeats each card's badge and folder count. `Packs.KINDS`/`LABELS` are shared with the reveal.

## Reviews

Two independent reviews. **Standards** found no must-fix; fixed from it: DESIGN.md and this report, `State.copyCap` reuse, comment wording, test title, a misplaced comment, Escape. **Spec** marked boxes 1, 3, 4 and 6 met and 2 and 5 partial; fixed: the drifting folder count (saved count above, plus a "no longer in your Collection" note), the card scrolling out of view at 200% zoom, the stall showing through, a stale Skip comment in `packs.js`, the unused `describe`, and an unguarded `Rift.PackReveal` call. Accepted: `stopSounds` also cuts the Skip/Done click (Skip is meant to be silent); focus after Done falls to the page when every pack button is disabled (unchanged from #136).

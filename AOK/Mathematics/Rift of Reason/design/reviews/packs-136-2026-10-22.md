The receipt (`wallet.pack`) holds the pack number, pool version, source, price, every card (kind, ID, rarity, foil, creature UID), any colour spells a first creature of a colour brought (`unlocked`, the existing catch rule) and a `done` flag. Only the latest receipt is kept; `wallet.packSerial` never goes down. `wallet.freeCards` holds free cards left over from a free pack opened with little room. Backup codes check its shape. No new storage key, no migration (the save defaults fill old wallets).

## Builder calls

- **Today's pool.** Only cards with a `release` and an explicit `packEligible: true` are in packs: the seven foundation-1 commons (six creatures and Small Patch). Older shipped cards carry no flag and stay out until someone releases them deliberately. So the odds honestly read "Common 100% (7 kinds)" and the pool holds 28 copies for a new student. Every rarity rule is tested on a synthetic release.
- **Free pack with partial room.** It opens the cards that fit and keeps the rest as saved free cards (the confirmation says so). They open first once there is room. With no room at all, nothing is used.
- **Reroll resistance.** A plain weighted pick reshuffled every card when one unrelated folder changed (found by the Spec review: restore an older backup, release one copy, buy again). Per-ID seeded keys keep the same odds but only change a pick when the winner itself changes.
- **One unseen pack at a time.** A second purchase is refused until Done, so the receipt is never overwritten unseen.
- **Foil rate** is its own versioned pack setting (0.04), not read from the catch setting, so retuning catches never changes pack rules silently.
- **Confirmation** before every pack (Cancel changes nothing), like the #135 sale review.
- **Done** marks the receipt shown. Until then the HUD button reads "Shop · … · new cards" and opening the stall shows the same saved list again.

## Reviews

Two independent reviews (Standards, Spec) found no must-fix. Fixed from them: keyboard focus after Cancel/Done (no redraw before the confirmation; focus returns to the same or a live button), card names instead of IDs in errors, a real Cancel in the #133 wallet test, the reroll hardening and partial free packs above, the one-unseen-pack rule, missing tests (pool version, legacy legendaries, partial free pack), the colour-spell list on the receipt, a pack status line beside the pack buttons, shorter student text, "<0.1%" for tiny odds, and stricter receipt checks (rarity, free price 0, unlocked list, freeCards).

## What plays

The camera travels down into the village, its scanner finds the player's home, and the warm sleeping-bedroom image contrasts with the impersonal targeting overlay. The morning crack stays thin: the sky does not open until the Fair. The film uses the player's nickname and the correct sleeper/surprised art for all ten avatars. ## Implementation and fixes

- Classic-script timeline `data/cutscenes.js`, DOM/rAF player `js/ui/cutscene.js`, stylesheet and no-save `dev/cutscene.html` bench. All scripts/styles are loaded by the main page.
- Script step `{cutscene:'cold-open',fallback:[...]}` keeps the original two lines on black when the player, required asset, network load or renderer fails. A 12-second art deadline prevents indefinite loading.
- First encounter routing initially cancelled the film immediately: `screen:changed` also fires after the new screen mounts. Removed that inappropriate listener. Navigation already cancels through `Dialogue.abortAll()` before the next mount.
- An aborted film now leaves its old dialogue suspended, matching the existing navigation contract. It cannot mark the opening seen, resume old wake-up speech or run old story rewards.
- Skip button, Escape and 900ms hold stop voice, clear timers, cancel the frame and release input once. Fixed the frame loop scheduling another frame after a hold-triggered Skip.
- Errors inside a later animation frame return the fallback instead of leaving a stuck overlay.
- Previous scene/tension, including silence, are restored. Previous connected focus is restored. The film is a modal input owner, with the shared game's keyboard focus handling.
- Calm motion and OS reduced motion use still holds/slow fades, no flashes, bolts, sweep, glitch or scrolling data. The quiet opening hold retains its duration. Every sound routes through the existing volume settings.
- A seen-gated **Watch the opening again** button in Journal replays only the film, deduplicates clicks and does not change progress or award anything. Existing `seen:prologue.cold` is the only receipt; backups stay unchanged.

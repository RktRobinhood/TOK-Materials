`State.transact` saves all stock, Coins and the latest receipt before publishing. Failure leaves live inventory/balance/receipt unchanged; stale-tab conflict checks preserve the other tab's write. Its receipt survives reload and both backup formats. A serial remains after reversal, so an old review cannot become valid again. Only one latest receipt is stored, with no accumulating sale log.

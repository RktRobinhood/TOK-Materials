# #158 slices 1–2: Pocket Nets rebuilt (JezzBall-style) and Shard Burst (Asteroids-style)

22 October 2026 · Paperclip FAL-256 (Claude). Teacher decisions of 18 October: rebuild Pocket Nets as a real-time wall game with terrain, add an Asteroids-style fifth game, fix the reported 2×3 trap, and give real-time games a deliberate Calm version. Slice 3 (one-line rules and win-rate tuning for Trail Finder, Lantern Lure and Picture Catch) is still to do, so #158 stays open.

## The reported bug, reproduced first

The teacher's report: an early cut boxed the creature into a small area (for example 2×3), and then the game could not be won. With the old turn-based engine, every 2×3 room reached by one or two clean cuts was unwinnable, even with casts left: **120 of 120** (300 seeds, common and rare). In a 2×3 room the creature moves three steps per cast and always crosses any cut, and the goal was 3 tiles. Reproduce with `node tools/repro-old-net-158.mjs`, which loads the old engine from commit c180d1c in git history. Summary in `catch-arcade-158-2026-10-22.json` → `oldNetRepro`.

Fix in the new game: **a creature boxed into six squares or fewer is penned, and its pen counts as closed ground.** Each extra creature in the same pocket allows three more squares, so a crowded pocket is penned too (Spec review: 3–4 portraits in 9–12 squares could otherwise break every net). A portrait in a one-square lane holds the lane's centre line instead of bouncing between its walls 12 times a second, which read as jitter. Regression tests (`tools/test/catch-arcade.test.mjs`) build a 2×3 rock pocket, close its doorway, and check four things: the pocket becomes a pen, the creature stays inside for 20 s, the meter counts it, and a winning line still exists from that state. Penning every creature wins at once.

## What shipped

| | Pocket Nets | Shard Burst |
|---|---|---|
| Field | 12×9 meadow; 3–5 rock/tree/pond clumps; one connected field | 12×9 night sky; edges wrap; lantern in the centre |
| Movers | the visitor's portrait, bouncing diagonally: 2/3/4/5 by rarity | 1 big + 1 medium / 2 big / 2 big + 1 medium / 3 big; big → 2 medium → 2 small each |
| Input | drag (the direction picks across or up–down) or tap (uses the ↔/↕ toggle, R); arrows + Space; P pauses | tap, click or hold toward the pointer; ←/→ aim + Space; P pauses |
| Lose | a creature touching a growing half breaks it: −1 spare net (3); or 60 s pass | a shard touching the lantern: −1 shield (4), with a 1 s grace; or 35 s pass |
| Win | close 80% of the field (pens count) | break every shard |
| Help (Great Charm, lure, Fox each) | +1 spare net and +10 s | +1 shield and +10 s |

- **Rules on screen:** one line of text. Pocket Nets shows a ghost net before Start (a clean first net that uses terrain), and under the pointer or keyboard cursor: a dashed line, with the ground it would close shaded. A red outline means "not here". Shard Burst shows a dotted aim line from the lantern. The HUD shows spare nets or shields, a progress meter with the goal mark, and the time left.
- **Calm motion / reduced motion:** step mode. The field waits, with each mover's next second drawn as dots from the same clock. It moves half a second after each move or **Wait** (W). A move that must wait for the last net works as Wait. No flashing, shaking or looping animation in either mode.
- **Saves:** a board is JSON plus an input log on a fixed 60-tick clock (`js/core/catch-arcade.js`). Each net or throw, each lost net or shield, every 5 s, and the end are saved through `CatchSession.play`. Replaying the log is the only authority. A reload resumes at the last saved tick, paused, with the same layout. It cannot undo a loss or reroll the game. Charging, the direct catch (with the existing foil roll), the copy-cap refund and backups are unchanged. Save **version 7** refunds an unfinished paid try of the old turn-based Pocket Nets once and keeps finished receipts; the visitor keeps Pocket Nets as its game.
- **Selection:** five games, uniform per attempt (the existing seeded stream). Rebound stays retired.

## Tuning and measured machine win rates

Every served board is admitted only if a **skilled test player** wins it unassisted against the exact clock; that run is the replayable certificate. All 3,200 held-out boards below are certified.

Rates were tuned against a documented **average sensible player**, then measured on held-out seeds (`node tools/sim-catch-arcade.mjs 400 design/reviews/catch-arcade-158-2026-10-22.json`):

- **Pocket Nets:** looks 0.6–1.5 s between nets, weighs six random spots, skips spots a creature seems to head for (speed misjudged up to 25%), reacts in 0.17–0.33 s and slips one cell 20% of the time.
- **Shard Burst:** holds the pointer down; picks the nearest shard 75% of the time; the pointer trails it by 0.25 s with about 6° of aim error and half a lead.

400 boards per row:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nets · common | 59.5% | 76.5% | 85.8% | 95.3% | 13.8% | 17.7 | 15.1 |
| Nets · uncommon | 59% | 75.5% | 88% | 94.5% | 4.5% | 21.7 | 17.9 |
| Nets · rare | 52.3% | 70% | 83.8% | 87.3% | 1.5% | 25.9 | 20.2 |
| Nets · legendary | 53% | 67.8% | 80.3% | 84.3% | 1% | 28 | 20.5 |
| Burst · common | 56.8% | 73% | 84% | 98% | 3.8% | 15.9 | 11 |
| Burst · uncommon | 52.5% | 72.3% | 87.8% | 95.5% | 0.5% | 17.6 | 12.5 |

Help never undid a win (checked on every board). Random play is a spot once a second in Pocket Nets and a throw five times a second in Shard Burst.

**Calm is easier, by design.** The step player acts once per half-second step, reads the exact one-second ghost path, and still slips or mis-aims a little. It wins 84–99%, against 42–60% in real time. That is the price of a version with no reaction time. The 18 October research note warned that a stepped mode "is not automatically equally hard". This is flagged for the teacher; slice 3 can shorten the ghost path or tighten Calm if it becomes a shortcut.

How it was tuned, briefly:

- **Pocket Nets.** The first settings (7 squares/s growth, 70% goal) gave 6–11 s rounds and 70–100% wins. Final: growth 4.5 squares/s, goal 80%, pen ≤ 6, speeds 2.95/2.55/2.4/2.3 with 2/3/4/5 portraits.
- **Shard Burst.** With three nets in the air, holding fire toward the shards won about 98%, and random spraying won 45–75% at common. Aim did not matter. Final: **one net in the air**, 4 shields, shard pace 2.8/2.42/2.18/2.02. Random spray now wins 0–4%.

The tuning is behind `NET`/`BURST` in `js/core/catch-arcade.js`. The test `average sensible player wins about half the time` guards the bands (38–66% per game, each rarity 15–85%, help +8 points or more, median win 12–60 s).

These are machine players. They do not show that an average student wins half the time, or that the rounds feel 20–60 s or fun. Winning rounds take 16–30 s; Shard Burst losses end sooner (about 11 s), when shields run out.

## Real browser checks

Headless Brave, isolated profile, `dev/catch-adventure.html` (the real encounter, real `CatchSession` saves under a separate storage prefix). All input was real mouse events through the DevTools protocol; page state was only read.

- **Pocket Nets, 1280×900:** Start charged one charm. Five real drags built nets; input reached the saved board within 44 ms. Caught; one creature, 8 charms; still caught after reload. No console errors.
- **Shard Burst, 1280×900:** reload at tick 300 resumed paused at the saved tick 300 with 4 shields. Holding and tracking with the mouse caught in 16 s with 23 throws; still caught after reload.
- **Pocket Nets, Calm, 390×844 (mobile emulation):** 2 s of frames before any move kept the clock at 0; one Wait moved exactly 30 ticks. Reload mid-round resumed at the saved tick. Caught. No horizontal overflow (scroll width = viewport).
- **Shard Burst, Calm, 1280×900:** the first run exposed a stall: a throw while the last net was still flying did nothing, so time stuck. Fixed (it now works as Wait) and covered by a test. Rerun: 2 s of frames kept the clock at 0; one Wait moved 30 ticks; reload resumed at the saved tick 300 with 4 shields; caught at tick 1631 (27 s of game time) after 45 taps; still caught after reload; no console errors.

Screenshots: `catch-arcade-158-net-ready.png`, `catch-arcade-158-net-mid.png`, `catch-arcade-158-burst-mid.png`, `catch-arcade-158-net-calm-phone.png`, `catch-arcade-158-burst-calm.png`.

Shard Burst replays use `Math.sin`/`cos`/`atan2`. A mid-round backup moved to a different browser engine could, rarely, replay a round in progress differently; settled receipts are unaffected. Pocket Nets uses only exact addition.

Not verified: physical touch hardware (pointer events from CDP mouse only), a real OS reduced-motion setting, sound, classroom players.

## Student flow and rules

Numbered Host/Join steps explain the outgoing code and return code. Native WebRTC uses no signaling service, STUN/TURN, account or backend. Text copy/paste is always available. Vendored local QR generation/scanning is optional; dense codes retain the text fallback. Scanning starts only on request and stops on cancellation, timeout, connection or departure. Frames are neither stored nor uploaded. Role, checksum and expiry checks still apply. Vendor versions, licences, integrity and byte hashes are in `js/vendor/README.md`.

The interface renders recipient-private views: own hand, legal actions/targets/choices, board, rules, held Axiom costs, public Win progress, powers, Fate and history. Selection and re-render retain keyboard focus; finished results receive focus. Actions carry attempt, match and revision identity. Guest DTOs are validated before display. Taxed Axiom prices come from authority. A completed result survives the partner leaving.

The host owns the monotonic 60-second deadline. Required choices/reactions consume that same turn's time; expiry resolves legal choices and ends it. The UI countdown cannot extend a turn. Background catch-up, duplicate/late/stale input, interruption, partial frames, bounded buffering and departure are covered by protocol tests. Lost connections end practice; retry starts a fresh match. The host is trusted with full match state; remote collection ownership cannot be authenticated without a service.

Practice never changes cards, copies, items, coins, statistics or rewards and calls no save writer. There is no resumable settlement or reward transaction. Local/ghost play remains available when peers cannot connect.

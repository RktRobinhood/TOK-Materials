# Card rules text readable everywhere — #163 slice 1

23 October 2026 · Paperclip FAL-270 · Claude. Card part of #163 only; the art and world-screen slices come later, so #163 stays open.

Matt: "Also ensure legibility of the cards. I feel like the text box gives issues." He was right. Before this change most card faces cut their rules off, and the type went down to 7–9.5 px.

## Targets

- No rules text is clipped anywhere it is shown. Where a face only has room for a short version, the short text says the same thing, and the full text is one tap or click away.
- Rules text is at least 12 px at 1280 px and at least 11 px at 390 px.
- Rules text has at least 4.5:1 contrast against its panel.
- The frames keep their look. No new art.

## How it was measured

`tools/measure-card-text.mjs` drives headless Firefox over WebDriver BiDi (there is no Playwright on this machine). It runs on `dev/card-text.html`, which is the production page with a memory-only save. It renders **every released card** (69 creatures, 68 tactics, 72 Axioms) in each place that shows rules text, at 1280×720 and 390×844:

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| big-card | The 300 px framed creature card: Collection details and the hunt reward (`Rift.UI.framedCard`). |
| pack-print / pack-caption | The pack reveal. Before: rules on the card face. After: rules in the caption under it. |
| arena-hand | Card Arena hand faces. |
| arena-board | Creatures on the table. |
| magnifier-card / magnifier-notes | The big card on hover, focus or long-press (the Read dialog and the opponent's reveal use the same face), and the notes under it. |

For each card it records:

- **Clipped:** a text line outside a box that hides overflow, a line clamp or ellipsis that cuts words, or a line off the screen.
- **The smallest font size** in CSS px.
- **Contrast:** two shots of one card per frame, with the text and without it. The text colour is compared with the pixels **under the letters only**, and the worst 5% is reported. The first version sampled the whole line box, so a box that brushed a border or name plate was blamed for pixels no letter covered.

The "before" run is `origin/main` (`9ae0735`) with the same bench and tool. The raw JSON is in `_scratch/fal-270/{before2,after3}`, which is not committed.

## What changed (builder calls)

1. **Hand cards print one word, at 12 px.** A creature shows its first ability's timing ("Entrance", "Last Word", "Activate"), else its first keyword, else the ability's icon. A tactic shows "Tactic". An Axiom shows "Rule", "Basic rule" or "Own rule". Before, hand faces carried a 3-line clamp of 9–11 px text that was cut off on 174 of 209 cards. The full text is in the magnifier, the Read dialog and the notes, as before.
2. **The table shows icons only.** Keyword and ability icons, no words. The old 7–8 px chips are gone. The words are in the magnifier.
3. **Big Arena cards are 13 px and at least 190 px wide.** That covers the magnifier, Read (170 → 190 px) and the opponent's reveal (minimum 130 → 190 px). A rule line is printed on the face **only if it fits** its box (`Rift.CardText`). Otherwise the face names what the card is, and the full rule is in the notes beside it:
   - A tactic shows "Reason · uncommon" (or "Tactic").
   - An Axiom shows its kind: "Card activations rule", "Back to normal: …" or "Your own card".
4. **Text stays on the frame's plate.** The tactic box is inset to the parchment plate Artist measured in FAL-271 (L20 R16.5 T71 B11.5 %), and the colour label left the tactic face so it has room. That frees 3 lines, and 47 of 68 tactics still print their short rule. The Axiom box is kept clear of the key and chain on the left of its plate (L22 R11 T70.5 B13 %). The tactic magnifier was at 1.02:1, with dark ink on the copper border; it now measures at least 9.6:1.
5. **The Collection big card is 300 px (was 220).** The longest ability (145 characters) fits in 4 lines of 12 px. The notes sit beside the card and wrap under it on a phone.
6. **The pack reveal prints the rules in its caption,** at 15–17 px, under the card. The card on the stage shrinks with the window, so it now carries only the name.
7. **Short windows keep 76 px hand cards.** A laptop with toolbars is 851 px or wider and 500–620 px tall. That layout used to shrink hand cards to 54–68 px, where "Last Word" (61 px at 12 px bold) would be cut. The table cards shrink instead, inside the existing 44 px minimum. Checked at 1280×500, 1280×560, 1366×620 and 900×600: the hand stays in view and no word is clipped.
8. **The Recent tile shows the full rule.** An opponent's tactic or Axiom whose rule doesn't fit its face is one click away: the newest Recent tile's notes now print the card's full rule. This only happens for moves you may see, so nothing private leaks.
9. **Nine short rules now say everything the card does.** Each one used to leave out an effect or a limit:

   | Card | Short rule now |
   |---|---|
   | Occam's Razor | "…abilities, keywords, boosts" |
   | Look Closer | "+ Elusive" |
   | Clear View | "+ Elusive, Shield" |
   | Label It | "it can't attack next turn" |
   | Persuasion | "it arrives asleep" |
   | Echoing Encouragement | "3 rounds" |
   | Scheduled Speech | "for a turn" |
   | Empty Set | "Abilities and keywords OFF" |
   | Limited Memory | "Hand limit 3: oldest cards go" |

   The `flux-*` shorts still leave out "for BOTH players" and the bounds. Every Axiom applies to both players, and the bounds are in the notes.

## The guard

`tools/test/card-text.test.mjs` has 7 tests. Node cannot lay out a page, so it checks a **measured budget** instead, and says so in its header.

`js/ui/card-text.js` holds the following, measured in Firefox (`--glyphs`) and checked against browser line counts:

- each box's width, type size and number of lines;
- the advance width of every Nunito letter the cards use.

An unknown letter counts as wider than "W". A word wider than its box never fits, because the browser would not break it.

The test fails if any of these happens:

- A released card's rules overflow a box: every creature ability on the 300 px card; every hand face; every big face on the plate it is drawn on.
- A big tactic or Axiom face is left empty.
- The notes miss the full rule.
- Any CSS rule that sizes card rules text goes under 12 px, or clamps or ellipsises it.
- Any box, card size, line height or hand width that the budget was measured at changes. If that happens, measure again and update the budget.

## Art findings (for Artist, FAL-271)

- **Tactic frames are the problem art.** The parchment plate is about 64 % × 19 % of the card, sits off-centre, and the top gem pokes into it. Tactics carry the longest rules. With the text inset to the plate, 21 of 68 tactic short rules don't fit 3 lines at 13 px; those faces show the colour and rarity, and the full rule is in the notes. A **taller, centred plate with no gem** (Artist's offer on FAL-270) would let more of them print on the face. This is recommended as the next art slice; `BOXES.tactic` would then be re-measured.
- **The Axiom frame** has a key and chain that cut about 20 % into its plate on the left. The text is now kept clear of them. A plate without the chain would give back about 10 % of the width.
- **Creature colour frames are fine:** 10.5:1 or better on the 300 px card and 4.6:1 or better on the hand word (Imagination frame, lilac "Last Word").

## Left as is

- **Hover magnifier notes at 390 px.** With a mouse at 390 px, the notes slide under the lower panel. This was the same before. On a phone the path is long-press or **Read**, and the Read dialog shows the whole rule (checked).
- **Table icons in the shortest windows.** At a 44 px table card, two icons fit and a third (a gained ability) can be cut. These are icons, not words; the magnifier names them.
- **"Off" ability lines** (struck through, opacity 0.5) are disabled text and were not measured for contrast.

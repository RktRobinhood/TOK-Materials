### Regressions and remaining problems

**A1. Medium. Ch2 Square: the suspicion choice now contradicts the puzzle the player has just solved.** `STORY.md:276` (beat 3), `:318` (the choice), `:328–332` (Fox and Moth-kin blind spots).
- The logic fix made the Square table explicit and solved: "nobody here is an imp", the Mayor included. Then the suspicion choice offers "The Mayor.", and four of five voice-marked options pick him. The Fox blind spot ("The Mayor peels off his face") plays at the Square too. After a solved table, these read as the voices being stupid, not tempted, and the blind spot is no longer wrong "in its own way".
- The beat is also now the longest required scene in Ch2: speech, the loaf squabble (3 lines), the table, the fib, three Quill lines, a choice and a hum.
- **Fix:** play the blind spots during the loaf squabble, *before* the table. Reword the choice's prompt to "Who's behind the feast?", so "The Mayor." is half-right (he signed it). That fits the Sundial's question as it is. Cut the squabble to two lines: "Mrs Crumb: Somebody took my loaf." / "Mayor: Not me! Smudge!"

**A2. Medium. Fin's turn is now an explanation, not a character beat.** `STORY.md:431`, `:478`.
- "It only brings out its trophies when it thinks it's winning. So I made it think so." makes the plan clear, but "Licence gone. Worth it." was cut to fit the stack. His reversal now ends on two lines of reasoning.
- "It" has no clear antecedent after "Courts do." A non-native reader may think "it" is the vault or the court.
- "Trophies" collides with the Thinking Trophy, the hero's want. In Ch3 the word now means the shadow piece.
- **Fix:** keep the six-line cap by folding the flourish into line 2: *"The Algorithm only shows off its prizes when it's winning. So I let it win. For a bit."* Change the Count 1 line to "LET THE JURY SEE MY PRIZE." The silent tie-straightening stays.

**A3. Low–medium. Every "Previously…" now repeats the same boast line word for word.** `STORY.md:153, 265, 378, 496`.
- A class that jumps in each lesson hears "At the Fair you said you'd win the Thinking Trophy. 'I always know the answer.' It heard." in three or four recaps. The logic fix turned an arc line into a reminder.
- **Fix:** keep the quote and escalate the tail. Ch2: "It heard." · Ch3: "It's been quoting you ever since." · Ch4: "It's building a better you. Up there."

**A4. Low. The Summit Rift question muddles the last word.** `STORY.md:581, 616`.
- "It used to say 'probably'. Then nobody clapped. Would you stop too?" is a yes/no question asked two beats before the avatar says "Probably.". A reader can take the last word as an answer to it ("probably I'd stop"), which is the opposite of the meaning. It also repeats core line 1 almost word for word.
- **Fix:** ask something that the Sundial's own last line answers: *"Nobody clapped for its honest word. Who claps for yours?"* Then "Tick. Tock. Good. Say it like that." becomes the clap.

**A5. Low. Small voice slips introduced or left by round 4.**
- `UNDERSTUDIES.md:115` (and §4.5): the Sequins column still has the old shelf line. Tally's "This one will always say 'probably'" is a certainty claim, and it comes one line before "I can't predict that. Nobody can." Try: *"I'd never sell it. I've labelled it. Neatly. He'd have used glitter."*
- `STORY.md:433`, tier 4: Nudge's "…Why is nobody cheering?" uses up the "first question" that its arc saves for the Fair ("…Why?"). Try: *"…Nobody's cheering. That isn't in the numbers."*
- `STORY.md:540`: "STEP ASIDE." (core) and then "Step aside. I'll answer." (the Copy) are two lines apart. Cut the core's.
- `STORY.md:560, 573`, Raven path: the box says "'Know' was the word they clapped for." and the voice says "You said 'know'. You meant 'guess'." back to back. Give the Raven voice-back a different word: *"Small word, 'guess'. Fits in a box."*

### Did the logic fixes flatten the story?

Mostly not. The clock rules, mid-boss fallbacks and charm rules went into the appendices, where they belong. The new on-page lines are mostly good: "Watch the table." keeps its dignity, "Only mine." is better than the old silent charm, and "UPLOAD COMPLETE · WAITING" is good menace. Ch4 does not sag: the Copy's bar gives the floors a pulse, and its first push at the Oracle door ("your own words in the wrong mouth") is the best new set piece. Three places did lose something, B1–B3.

### Regressions and remaining problems

**B1. Moderate. The Ch2 Square is overloaded, and its choice fights the table.** `STORY.md:276, 318`.
- Since round 4 the Square holds a speech, a scene-only squabble, a fixed table, a fib, Quill's clue, a choice and a hum. It is the "fourth event at one station" shape again.
- Worse, the suspicion choice comes right after a table that proved the Mayor is no imp. A reader asks "suspect him of what?", and the voices that pick him look silly rather than misled.
- **Fix:** as A1. Blind spots go before the table, and the prompt becomes "Who's behind the feast?". Trim the squabble to two lines.

**B2. Moderate–low. The misjudged villain's payoff is correct but cold.** `STORY.md:431`.
- The reveal now explains, and then the screens delete his licence in silence. The emotional beat that sold him in round 3 ("Worth it.") is gone. The reader understands Fin but has no line to love him for.
- **Fix:** as A2: *"…So I let it win. For a bit."* Use "prize", not "trophy", so the Thinking Trophy keeps its word.

**B3. Low. Recaps say the same line in every chapter.** `STORY.md:153, 265, 378, 496`.
- Escalate the tail each time (A3). A recap should feel like "previously, and worse", not a reminder.

**B4. Low. Story 7's twist is over-clued.** `SIDE-STORIES.md:275, 282, 292`; also story 1, `:83`.
- The hook already says "his toy army". Story 1 shows the same "toy army" with cork rifles. The object clue gives three tells ("never blink", "eyes don't glow", "paint chip"). The Moth-kin and Raven hooks both point at the front rank.
- A skim-reader has the twist from the hook.
- Story 7's ripple (the soldiers "hold the far end of the Summit Rift") is never seen on screen.
- **Fix:**
  - In story 7's hook, say "his regiment". Keep story 1's toy soldiers as the fair plant.
  - Give the object clue only the paint chip and "never blink".
  - At the core, when the drain applies, show one silent image: a wooden soldier wedged in the Summit Rift door, drum raised.

**B5. Low. `UNDERSTUDIES.md` is stale in two places.**
- `:9` says "Binding facts from STORY.md (round 3)".
- `:115`: the shelf row predates STORY.md's new Sequins line, and Tally's "always" fights the Sundial's "Nobody can". Fix as A5.

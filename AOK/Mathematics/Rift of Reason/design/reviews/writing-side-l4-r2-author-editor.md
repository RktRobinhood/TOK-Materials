## Remaining problems (all small; none blocks the pass)

1. **fortune-machine · round 3 wrong reply (the reference-class fix) · wordy and abstract.** "Its ninety is about all its fortunes. Mostly ordinary days. Not about him." "Its ninety is about…" is hard to parse, and "Not about him" sits awkwardly next to the correct answer, which *is* about him (yesterday). Say the reference class in Mirage's voice:
   → `Ninety is the box's score, darling. Not the hedgehog's chance.`

2. **giveaway-app · outcomes 1 and 2, ACHILLES · he runs off, then speaks.** The note "…He runs off before anyone can thank him." is followed by GRIEF, his spoken line. Put `GRIEF` before the Achilles note in both outcomes:
   - outcome 1: `…'Like and subscribe.'` → GRANNY note → `GRIEF` → `Achilles carries a basket to Volt. He runs off before anyone can thank him.`
   - outcome 2: the same order, with the soft-berry note.

3. **giveaway-app · start · Speedcheeta's reply ignores the accusation.** "I don't even like berries! I like WINNING!" now answers "Are you HACKING my app?" with a non sequitur. Make the denial part of the line:
   → `HACKING? I don't even like berries! I just like WINNING!`

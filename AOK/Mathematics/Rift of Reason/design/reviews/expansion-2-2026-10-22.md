## Tests

`tools/test/expansion-2.test.mjs` adds 14 tests. They cover:

- **Each hook:** equal-attack choice, aura-adjusted comparison, Elusive excluded, Greta's heart threshold, Nelly's exact deck order and private event text, Ryan's expiry turn by turn, both Pika modes, Share the Feeling's expiry, death and Shield, and both timed keywords with no doubling.
- **Empty source and no target:** a 0- or 1-card deck, no equal enemy, no enemy, and a spell with no friendly creature (not offered).
- **Loop and re-entry:**
  - Empty Set turns off all four new Entrances, Ryan's included. Ryan then asks for no target. Nothing fires late after the rule leaves and a turn passes.
  - Rethink then replay needs a fresh paid play and a fresh activation. This is checked for Grant, Nelly (one more look at the new top two) and PewDiePika (the boost is cleared, then one new boost).
  - Two Ryans on one target give −2 with the same end, and the second finds nothing to lose on a 1-attack target.
  - Share the Feeling cast twice gives 2 damage and +4, and both end together.
  - Warm Blanket cast twice heals twice but keeps one timed Shield, as Remember the Guard keeps one Guard.
  - An AI self-play fuzz checks that the game ends and every card stays in one zone.
- **Information:** previews and all three AI levels choose the same action when the hidden deck order changes.
- **Sources and saves:** catch weights, pack roster and rarities, story exclusion, four-copy cap, and copies (normal and foil) surviving a packed backup code.
- **Metadata:** runtime data matches the catalogue, handlers are real, and portraits and motifs exist in the asset manifest.

14/14 pass. Log lines now say so when nothing happened: a 0-attack Ryan target "has no attack to lose", and a creature that already had Guard or a Shield is told so.

Three existing tests depended on the old pool, so they were updated:

- **Pack odds.** The pack-odds tests read the old "Common 100% (7 kinds)" pool. They now derive weights from the real roster and check the new pre-unlock odds.
- **Reveal badge.** The reveal test now matches the saved card's own tier badge.
- **Hard vs Easy.** "Hard usually beats Easy" draws random teams from every creature, so the new species changed its sample to 22/40 against a 24/40 bar. This change was made so the batch could pass, so here is why it is safe. With the new hooks removed, the same seeds give 21/40. Other seed sets give 28–32/40, and without the batch they give 24–29/40. That makes the 40-game sample noise, not an AI regression. The test now plays 80 games against the same 60% bar (48/80); measured results are 52–63/80 with the batch and 49–63/80 without.

**Full suite** (under the 12 GB memory cap): 1,432 tests, 1,431 pass, 1 skip, 0 fail.

`validate-planning.mjs` now checks all three runtime batches and asserts that 177 are held. `validate-personas.mjs` checks the names and that no held creature has a runtime definition. Both pass, and the regenerated catalogue, persona, roster and review files are byte-stable.

## Paired mono/mixed games and the mirror

`node tools/sim-expansion-2.mjs 16` (and `--without-batch-hooks`) plays 16 seeds × 2 seats per Way at Competent vs Competent. Each run is 192 mono-vs-mixed games plus 192 **mono mirror** games: the same deck on both sides, so any split there is seat, first-player and seed noise. All decks are legal 20-card personal decks with the ten shared Axioms. The batch card leads each Way's deck. Memory keeps John Greenfinch as its body because its new card is a spell. The mixed deck uses its own Way plus the next one. Reports: `expansion-2-2026-10-22/mono-mixed-probe.json` and `mono-mixed-without-hooks.json`.

**The control.** With hooks off, the creatures keep their bodies, costs and keywords (Greta keeps Guard) and lose only the new hook. Each batch spell becomes a plain heal 2 on its friendly target, the way #123 kept Small Patch, so no control deck carries dead cards.

**Noise.** Across the 12 mirror rows, seat 0's share runs from 0.31 to 0.59, which is up to ±0.19 around even. Binomial noise agrees: one 32-game share has a standard deviation of about 0.09, so a difference between two shares has about 0.125, and two standard deviations is about ±0.25.

**Reading.** All six changes are inside that noise. Emotion, Perception and Memory move by 0.03. Imagination moves +0.13. Language (−0.19) and Reason (−0.15) move the most, and both move towards parity. Their new creature also sits in the mixed decks (Reason with Emotion, Language with Imagination), so the mixed side gains a card too, which #123 asked later batches to do. Sixteen seeds cannot show this effect for certain. The large skews (Perception mono weak, Memory and Reason mono strong) are already there with the hooks off. They come from the fixture decks, not this batch. No numbers were tuned. Like #123, this probe uses one opponent Way per colour. A second opponent Way is left to the next batch (below).

**Plays and bounds.** Every new card was played in the hooks-on run: Warm Blanket 202, Grant 169, Greta 135, PewDiePika 123, Ryan 119, Nelly 106, Remember the Guard 67, Share the Feeling 60. Every action kept each card in one zone. No hooks-on game reached the turn limit; the longest took 144 actions. The one hooks-off Reason draw was a turn-limit game; no mirror game was drawn. The Competent AI seldom spends a turn on a defensive spell in a small mixed fixture, so the in-suite fuzz asserts creature plays only.

## Browser verification

- **Battle board.** On `dev/expansion-2.html`, I ran the production board with a memory-only save in headless Firefox 158 over WebDriver BiDi, using real pointer input. Each of the 8 cards was committed at 1280×720 normal, at 1280×720 Calm (mouse), and at 390×844 Calm (touch taps). That is 24 commits, and all reached the expected state:
  - Grant stripped Lobstorian's Guard; the 1-attack Beastie was untouched.
  - Greta showed 3/5 Guard at 4 hearts, with the reader naming "Hold the Line".
  - Nelly's dialog showed the two cards to the player only; the log said "You put Proof Pup on the bottom".
  - Ryan took Beastie from 2 to 1 attack.
  - PewDiePika reached 2/3.
  - Share the Feeling, Remember the Guard and Warm Blanket hit their exact damage, attack and keyword outcomes.
- **Calm, width and errors.** Under Calm, no running animation used transform, translate, scale or rotate. The document width stayed 390 on the phone. The browser log had no errors or warnings.
- **Pack stall.** At the Campfire Clearing, real presses on the stall bought a five-card pack. It held Remember the Guard ×2, Share the Feeling, Tom Skink and John Greenfinch. The reveal played card by card, showing "Remember the Guard · New copy · Folder 1/4". This worked at 1280 and at 390 (touch).
- **Not verified in the browser:** a full chapter-two catch (spawn weights are tested), a physical touch screen, the OS reduced-motion setting and `file://`.

All reached the same outcomes, with no errors and no transform motion under Calm. Screenshots and `browser-results.json` are beside this report. The bench and scratch driver hold no real progress.

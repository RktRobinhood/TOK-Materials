## Remaining problems

1. **cake-tins and silent-pupil · resolve intro no longer matches round 1.** Both stories now open with a side question, but the intros still ask for the main answer.
   - Cake Tins: the intro "Pick a tin. Pull it out. Mind the custard." is followed by a question about "at most one". → intro: `First, my rule. Then a tin. Mind the custard.`
   - Silent Pupil: "Well, child? Name the writer. Or we stay until morning." / "So. Who was it?…" are followed by the Beansprout question. →
     - quill: `Well, child? First, the quiet one. Then name the writer.`
     - gumleaf: `So. Start with Beansprout. Then who wrote it. No pressure. Well, a little.`

2. **silent-pupil · round 1 correct reply · lectures.** Astrophysicat: "Well, actually: the board said something that can't be true or false. Beansprout said nothing at all." It has 18 words and only explains. Keep the distinction, and make it a joke:
   → `Well, actually: the board says too much. Beansprout says nothing. Different problems.`

3. **silent-pupil · outcome 3 · the punchline has no set-up.** The accusation line was cut, so "I sat nearest. That was not a proof. That was a distance." now answers nothing on screen.
   → note: `The class is freed, very late. The teacher blames Astrophysicat. He sits nearest the board.`
   → astrophysicat: `I sit nearest. That is not a proof. That is a distance.`

4. **cake-tins · Why (correct) · inverted and long.** "Only with the cake in B is at most one label true. A and C each make two true." Non-native readers will trip on "Only with … is …".
   → `Cake in B: only C's label is true. Cake in A or C: two are true.`

5. **cake-tins · Moth-kin inner line points at the answer (my own round 1 wording).** "Ooh, tin B gleams." B holds the cake, so the "shiny" trap leads to the right tin. Point it at the itching powder instead, which is Moth-kin's blind spot doing its job:
   → `Ooh, tin A gleams. Shiny means nothing. …The dent on C, though. Look.`

# Card minigame rebuild — 6 October 2026

The teacher's classroom playtest supersedes the original Mindbug-style battle section. Big power alone must not look like the goal. Students need a visible turn budget, choices about readiness, and changing starting rules that visibly change conclusions.

## Required behaviour

- A turn permits multiple actions and ends with an explicit End turn button.
- Each player's energy capacity grows 1 → 2 → 3 on their own turns, refills at the start, and caps at 10. Unspent energy does not carry over.
- Energy pays for playing creatures, activating abilities and changing axioms. Attacking costs no energy. Each of these uses one action.
- Start with three actions and six hearts. Axioms can change action allowance, costs, growth, draw, readiness, combat and victory. A lower action limit does not refund already spent actions.
- Attack, block and activate exhaust the creature until its controller's next turn. New creatures can block but ordinarily wait a turn to attack or activate. Passive effects remain visible and active; creatures with only passive skills can pay to Focus for +1 power, up to +3.
- Paid rewrites cost two energy and choose from three public offers. Both players share the changed rule. It persists until its category is replaced or all rules reset. Several different categories can apply together.
- Underdog makes lower combat power win. Reversed Hearts awards victory to the player whose own hearts reach zero. No creatures left remains a loss; the turn limit remains a draw. Every current rule and the actual victory goal must be readable during play.
- Predictable timeline: round 4 free flip, round 7 reset, then alternate every three rounds. Changes in the offers can change the next flip; forecast the currently available top card. Recycle discarded rules before announcing a flip, never silently promise an unknown card.
- Each creature's paid skill is an explicit action. Activating exhausts it. No free triggered skill on play. Normal matches have no steal-token reaction.
- Rebuild Granny's practice to guide real engine actions across several turns: play/pay, End/refill, attack/block/exhaust, another action, paid skill, reserve defence, paid Underdog and reversed-goal victory. Highlight the actual card/control and pace moves; respect calm motion.
- Keep collected creatures, practice safety, trainer stakes/fate and the adventure's existing puzzle prerequisites. Existing art is sufficient. Use the current text for narration fallback; old recordings must not speak a different tutorial.

## Validation and release

The teacher's follow-up adds a ten-card axiom contribution per player and a central Fate track. Collection must allow choosing ten distinct available rules; retain duplicate copies across the two contributions and shuffle all twenty deterministically. NPCs contribute a seeded selection; ghost codes carry the chosen rules; old saves receive a default selection. Fixed teaching/story decks remain explicit exceptions. Fate advances once per End turn, alternates flip/reset every six spaces, and shows the current countdown. Filter advances two spaces; Next Year delays two (maximum twelve away). A skill that reaches zero triggers the event immediately. Delayed spells/creature arrivals are future design possibilities, not part of this tested first slice.

Axiomatic is a paid creature skill offering five rules rather than the standard three; its activation pays for the rewrite. Program gains a skill but never triggers that copied paid skill automatically.

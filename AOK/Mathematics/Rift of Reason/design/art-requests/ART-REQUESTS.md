# Art requests for Rift of Reason (for ChatGPT)

One request per part of the art epic (#32). The teacher runs every request in **one ongoing ChatGPT chat**, so earlier images (the style board, approved sheets) are already in its context and **Attach** just names which earlier images the prompt leans on. Results are pasted into the Claude session, which critiques them and saves keepers under **Save as** (inside `design/source-assets/`).

Do **0.1 first**. Once it is approved, every other request attaches it as `style-board.png`, and the parts can be done in any order.

If a result is close but wrong, don't fix it yourself in the chat: bring it back, and Claude will write a corrected prompt. Prompts get revised in this file, so it always holds the latest version.

## Rules pasted into every prompt (already included below)

> Transparent background (PNG). No text, letters, numbers, labels, floor, or shadow blobs. Clear empty space between every figure so nothing touches or overlaps. All full-body figures at the same scale on one imaginary ground line, feet visible.

### 0.1 Style board

**Attach:** nothing.
**Save as:** `0-style/style-board.png`

**Prompt:**

> Create a style board for a cute-but-dark fantasy creature adventure game for teenagers. The world is a storybook creature land at dusk: whimsical, slightly spooky, never gory. Style: thick dark ink outlines of consistent weight, cel shading with a soft painterly texture, a little rim light, saturated jewel colours (magenta, emerald, crimson, violet, gold) glowing against deep forest-green and midnight-violet darkness, big expressive eyes, round friendly shapes. It should feel like a polished modern indie card game.
>
> One wide canvas (about 1536×1024) with three areas separated by empty space, no text anywhere:
>
> 1. **Left: the hero.** A small owlet child (a round, fluffy owl chick standing upright like a little person, big amber eyes, soft ear tufts, cream-and-brown feathers) wearing a very long red knitted scarf and a battered leather satchel. Three full-body poses, same scale, feet visible, on a transparent background: standing idle; walking mid-stride, facing right; startled, wings up.
> 2. **Top right: a character card.** A portrait-shaped card (5:7) with an ornate dark-magenta frame and small gold corner flourishes, an art window showing a grinning cat in a waistcoat patterned with galaxies, one paw raised as if saying "well, actually", and two empty text panels below the art (leave them blank). The cat is lit by a blue glow.
> 3. **Bottom right: a background swatch.** A rectangle of painted scenery: a lantern-lit fairground at the edge of a dark enchanted forest at dusk, glowing mushrooms, bunting, a crooked tent, a distant swirl of cyan light in the sky (a time rift). No characters.

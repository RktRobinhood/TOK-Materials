# Twenty-three future conditional concepts

Separate future expansion after the initial batch. These are additional proposals, not replacements or runtime definitions in the existing 210. Three per Way of Knowing plus five Generic. Costs (1–2), visibility, actor/trigger menus, targets, caps and timing are proposals for later review. All use the backend handoff: one committed trigger/actor, post-resolution reaction, consume before effect, ordinary energy/play limits, no free rearming. “Either” may include actor-neutral events only where the event schema allows them. Face-down choices remain committed, not rerolled on reveal.

Colour spells/conditional cards still obey the approved submitted-deck quota; no live matching-creature prerequisite. Generic helps mono and mixed plans without deciding the separate Memory/Generic classification. Exact once-per-turn mono/mixed bonuses are deliberately not added to these frozen proposals.

## Reason

### Premise Pending

- ID: conditional-reason-premise-pending; proposed cost 1; face-down.
- Arm: opponent; creature-played. Choose one printed option where a menu exists.
- Effect: The triggering creature loses 1 attack until its controller’s next turn ends, minimum 0.
- Role: Small precise ambush; preparing an equal-attack exchange.
- Counterplay: Play a low-value body first; an irrelevant/no-longer-present target still consumes the condition.
- Gates: 148, 109, 123.

### Spare Premise

- ID: conditional-reason-spare-premise; proposed cost 1; face-up.
- Arm: self; axiom-replaced. Choose one printed option where a menu exists.
- Effect: Your most damaged surviving creature gains 1 maximum health and heals 1. Ties use oldest board arrival.
- Role: Rule-change support for a durable board.
- Counterplay: Remove its useful recipient before the rule change; no creature means no effect.
- Gates: 148, 109, 123.

### Exhibit Aha

- ID: conditional-reason-exhibit-aha; proposed cost 1; face-down.
- Arm: opponent; creature-played / spell-played. Choose one printed option where a menu exists.
- Effect: Inspect your top 2 personal-deck cards; keep their order or swap them. Draw nothing.
- Role: Commit to one anticipated action; private preparation rather than a free draw.
- Counterplay: Force the unhelpful timing; deck mutation/draw limits still apply.
- Gates: 148, 109, 123.

## Validation and release notes

Arithmetic/identity checks:23 unique IDs, names and effect texts; six groups of3 plus5Generic; no ID/name collision with the 210 catalogue; bounded printed menus and costs. This is not engine testing or proof of balance. Each effect has a different trigger/target/payoff combination within its colour. Shared small damage, healing, Shield, recovery and tokens remain deliberate primitives, not a claim that every underlying operation is novel.

Before release, audit against the then-current shipped set, paid/copy/recovery loops, event ordering, private/public views, caps and active Win rules. Reject exact clones; do not silently change the 210 to make room. Shared trigger-back/armed-marker art, existing colour motifs/full-art normal frames and reused card-play/reveal/block/axiom/defeat sounds are future needs only. No portrait, sound or voice generation in this draft. Fate/expiry/replay/tie and conditional rearm policy remain explicit gates.

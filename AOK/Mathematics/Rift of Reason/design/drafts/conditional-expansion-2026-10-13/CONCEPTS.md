# Twenty-three advanced conditional concepts

Latest13October steering: after ALL current initial/starter generation, proceed directly to backend implementation and this set’s design/art. No implementation/generation in this handoff. These23 proposals are separate from the existing210:3 per Way plus5Generic, with8masked creatures/15spells. Each Way has aggro/midrange/control lines and leans hard into its specialism. Costs(1–2), creature stats, visibility, menus, ordering and wording remain proposals.

Campaign availability requires TWO completed lessons, recommended canonical history gate+b-town-hall; an NPC/quest introduces the set. Teasers grant nothing, old qualified saves infer history, qualification does not grant all23, and plot-only cards never enter packs. Shared Axioms, stars, rift jumps or current chapter do not substitute for completion.

Arming commits one printed trigger/actor. Spells consume/resolve/discard once. Masked creatures reveal/enter through normal handling and execute their printed Entrance once; no extra payment/free attack, and sleeping/Swift still applies. Seat Reserved introduces accepted pre-damage Guard interception, with board-space/reveal-event/target edge policies still gated. Other reactions remain post-action. Creature construction contributions and spell quotas are different; absent colour does not imply Memory. Optional mono/mixed themes are not silently injected stat bonuses.

Next implementation order:

1. **Done 17 October 2026.** Lifetimes tied to a controller's turn. A boost can last until a player's next turn ends (`untilTurn`, from #121) or starts (`untilStart`). A keyword can be gained or lost until that player's next turn starts (`timedKeywords`: never stacks, never changes the creature's own keywords, and a timed Shield is used up first and never revives a used printed one). Effects end at turn start, a bounce clears them, and Silence hides them. Damage is never touched. The engine hooks are `api.buffUntilStart` and `api.keywordUntilStart`, and "Active effects" names them. Premise Pending, Standing Ovation, Velvet Reveal, The Chair Recognises You and Spare Umbrella are playable on the bench. Tests: `tools/test/conditional-lifetimes.test.mjs`.
2. **Done 17 October 2026.**
   - Spare Premise offers only legal pairs of your other creatures with equal attack (a single pair is chosen automatically, none means no effect). Its trigger is your own Axiom change, so the question comes mid-turn.
   - Paper Tiger makes a bench-only colourless 1/1 paper token (`conditional-paper-token`). Like every token (#121) it respects reserved spaces, is never collected and never counts as a casualty.
   - Costume Rehearsal gives every paper token a timed Shield that never stacks.
   - **Builder decision (Wrong Way Round):** a temporary swap made of two boosts that end when the armer's next turn starts. Attack becomes the health left, and health left becomes the attack (max health = old attack + damage). Damage taken meanwhile stays when the swap ends. Max health never drops below 1.
   - Tests: `tools/test/conditional-imagination.test.mjs`.
3. **Done 17 October 2026.** Queue That Question uses a separate spell-only tax, `P.spellTax` (`api.spellTax`). The player's next spell card, including arming a conditional spell, costs 1 more. It is used up when paid, or ends with the controller's next turn (the #121 "next turn" convention). Creatures, rule cards and abilities are never taxed. A new tax replaces an unpaid one.
4. **Done 17 October 2026.** `enterSpace` raises a semantic fact `{ event: 'fate-entered', actor, space, how }` for each space Fate enters. The actor is the player who moved Fate (card, ability, draw or leap), or null for the ordinary round-end move, so only "either" conditions match that move. Reactions resolve after the whole movement. A skipped environmental occurrence is never replayed. Clock Eyes (a private look at the top 2 shared rule cards), Second Alarm and Round of Refreshments' Fate option are on the bench, which turns the Fate track on for them. **Builder decision:** Second Alarm moves your *soonest* waiting one-shot rather than asking, because a question asked at a round's end would skip the next draw (known limitation, FATE-SCHEDULE-MODEL.md section 8). It never moves an environmental event or a repeatable space, and never further than 4 rounds ahead. Tests: `tools/test/conditional-fate-entry.test.mjs`.
5. **Done 17 October 2026. Builder decision: no rearming.** `conditionSpent` still lasts the whole match. Borrowed Tomorrow puts your oldest conditional *creature* from your discard pile (never itself, never a token) on the bottom of your deck, as the same instance with its used condition. A used conditional spell could never be played again, so spells are not taken. The teacher can overturn this with a paid-rearm rule later.

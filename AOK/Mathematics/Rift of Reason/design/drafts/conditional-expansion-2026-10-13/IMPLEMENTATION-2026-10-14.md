# Conditional expansion: implementation decisions, 14 October 2026

Latest human instruction authorizes starting the backend and expansion art now. This supersedes the old execution wait; campaign release remains a separate completed-work check.

Implemented foundation: two armed slots, paid arming through normal play actions, at most three committed options, post-resolution spell/creature/Axiom facts, defeat facts, and pre-damage attack declarations. Masked creatures reserve board space and count toward remaining creatures. Reveal claims the condition before effects, enters normally, increments creature-play count once and runs Entrance once. Reveals invoke existing entry hooks but do not recursively emit creature-played conditional facts.

Attack counts are reserved once. After reveals and Entrance choices, the attacker chooses among legal Guards. Ignore-Guard rules still apply. Missing/frozen/departed attackers or vanished targets cancel the spent attack; there is no arbitrary retarget. A forced full-board failure consumes the masked card into its owner discard. Conditions resolve in arming sequence and stable ID order, with snapshot eligibility at the semantic event boundary. Choices pause that committed queue. Source consumption survives bounce/recovery for the whole match; spent spells cannot rearm and spent creatures replay normally.

Redundant Axioms emit no replacement fact; mass reset is one compound replacement. Facts retain actual paid cost, event sequence and original root action. Defeat actor is the controller before defeat, distinct from ownership. Either may match neutral replacement; self/opponent require a player actor. No Fate-entry facts or player occurrence scheduler are installed by this slice. Existing Win rules and simultaneous both-zero draw remain authoritative; terminal outcomes clear queued reactions. A 128-reveal diagnostic limit throws before the cloned action is committed, rather than silently discarding reactions.

Public rails expose hidden backs/counts; owner views and face-up records show identity/committed condition. The opponent arming animation suppresses concealed identities. AI lookahead receives a concealed-card projection. Preview labels warn that conditions may change combat. Offline engine records remain inspectable; this is presentation fairness, not online secrecy.

Ten cards are playable in the scratch developer bench only: eight spells plus Somewhere Safe and Seat Reserved. Generic creature colour contribution is explicitly none; Memory remains Memory. Footnote Trap is displayed as Footnote, Please, retaining its stable ID. Refreshments currently offers only Axiom replacement: its future Fate menu remains gated. The other thirteen concepts are unchanged drafts. No campaign grant, save migration, introduction, pack or deck release is claimed.

Finish before campaign release: remaining condition effects and lifetimes, token/reservation interactions, Fate/player occurrence policies, all-route two-lesson eligibility and saved introduction, initial construction quotas/board integration, art/readability/accessibility and balance review. Existing owned progress and recordings must remain untouched.

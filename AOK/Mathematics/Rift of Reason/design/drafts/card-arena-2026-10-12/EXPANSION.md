# Collection expansion — teacher direction, 12 October 2026

At least 200 additional cards. Planning target: **210**, with **28 per colour** (Reason, Emotion, Perception, Language, Imagination, Memory) and **42 generic cards (20%)**. Produce distinct concept/effect/counterplay drafts; the full catalogue is still to be written. It is additional to the small starter pack. Preserve multiple strategies and victory routes, with substantial Fate/Axiom interactions.

Rarity order requested: common, uncommon, rare, mythic, legendary. Proposed frames: grey, green, blue, violet, gold, with text/icon cues. Audit current tiers and introduce Mythic across all affected systems rather than merely colouring a frame. Common-heavy draft allocation: 92 common, 60 uncommon, 37 rare, 14 mythic, 7 legendary (210); proposed distribution, not approved pack odds.

Board redesign comes before major content implementation. Card creation should land in small playtested batches; do not mass-implement unbalanced concepts. Draft new creature/portrait/card art needs, reuse existing frames where useful, and share effect sounds. Avoid excessive bespoke audio.

Audit story reward frequency, collection pacing and access to new strategies. Catching/lure fun and replenishment get a separate playtest issue, including unlucky/low-score runs.

The pack/shop direction uses earned game currency. Audit existing systems before creating another currency. Each card progresses through two normal copies, then two foil upgrades, then leaves eligible pack pools. Shrinking eligible pools redistribute rarity odds; higher rarities remain possible early. Foils are cosmetic. Preserve caught-instance differences, legacy collections and spells currently unlocked as IDs; quantities/migration must not invalidate playable decks. All economy/progress belongs in the save/backup. Pack size, exact odds, pricing, sell safeguards, completed-pool behaviour and guarantees remain design decisions.

A full 210-card creative catalogue and a detailed economy model remain the next drafting tasks; they are not claimed complete by this brief.

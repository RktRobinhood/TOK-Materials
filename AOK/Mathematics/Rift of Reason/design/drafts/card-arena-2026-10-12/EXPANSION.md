## Latest content direction: unique, funny and broadly balanced

The teacher delegates these decisions to the builder. Each collectible should earn its place with a distinct play pattern and a funny ability that fits its public-persona joke. A different name, colour, animal or stat line alone does not justify a mechanical clone. Shared keywords and simple effect primitives are fine; their trigger, choice, timing, payoff or strategic role must create a meaningful distinction. Audit starter drafts as well as the expansion before implementation: several current vanilla starter bodies repeat 1/2 or 1/3 Guard patterns and need this pass. Keep beginner effects short, bounded and easy to teach.

Compare each proposed release against existing cards and the other drafts. Record its unique hook, persona/ability joke, supported strategy and counterplay. Merge redundant concepts or replace them with genuinely distinct cards; preserve the promised total and six-colour/generic coverage through replacements, not duplicate padding. Selected personas may need revised mechanics to strengthen their joke; update the authoritative draft and mapping together, retain stable IDs and document any tuning. The current 210 concepts are not claimed to have passed this uniqueness/balance audit.

Aim for several viable paths: creature pressure, Guard/control, attrition/recovery, Axiom changes, Fate planning/manipulation and alternate wins. This is mainly single-player; approximate strategy balance and enjoyable encounters matter more than tournament-level equality. Avoid one strategy making the rest pointless.

For each implemented batch, probe interactions with both existing and new cards: repeatable draw/energy/discount/recursion loops, zero-cost chains, replay duplication, token growth, unavoidable turn locks and trivial automatic wins. Put explicit use/expiry/resource bounds where needed, respecting approved alternate-win rules. Keep spectacular finite combos when opponents have meaningful responses. Test suspected exploits at the authoritative engine seam and in visible battles; do not claim balance from equal card counts or the pack-probability simulation.

# Initial reward and catching audit — planning evidence

12 October 2026. Source inventory only; #124/#125 still own complete run-level audits, interactive playtests and tuning. No statement here establishes that catching is already fun or balanced.

## Existing paths inspected

- Checked puzzle rewards give XP, a 50% base chance of a Catch Charm, a 15% chance of one battle-resource item and a 5% Trick Book chance. A first completion adds one guaranteed Catch Charm. Thus an ordinary **first** puzzle completion averages 1.5 Charms; a successful replay averages 0.5 before other rewards. This does not include actual visitor frequency or attempted catch rate.
- Miniboss rewards give two Charms and one choice drawn among tonic/Great Charm/lure, with other resources. Boss rewards give Great Charms and battle-support resources. First miniboss/boss victories teach an unowned earned tactic when one remains. A first trainer win also has an idempotent reward path for a Trick Book and an eligible tactic.
- First chapter-rift jumps grant three Charms and one tonic; an unowned common creature is added only while the player's collection has fewer than three creatures. A large owned starter grant will change the relevance of that safeguard; do not silently remove catch-up supplies.
- Current colour-spell unlocks depend on catching/owning a colour. Starting spell availability and first colour unlocks must be reviewed when owned avatar decks replace ordinary loan filling.
- Capture chance uses rarity base probabilities plus catch item/lure/avatar effects. A lure is a support item with a visit duration, not a catch-attempt replacement. Identify exactly when its visit counter is spent, especially cancelled visits, failures, retries and chapter jumps.
- Current map data has 26 dedicated puzzle nodes (3 prologue, 4 Ch1, 9 Ch2, 6 Ch3, 4 Ch4), four dedicated card-battle stations and four miniboss/four boss nodes. Optional station challengers and scripted encounters mean these counts are not a complete list of all battles or a promised shortest route.

## Why source counts are insufficient

Low-grade runs, repeated visits, catch skipping, avatar perks, visitor spawns, story gifts, difficulty choice and failed catch attempts all affect net inventory. The replay's 0.5 expected Charm supply can be below one Charm spent per repeated catch attempt; first-time guaranteed supplies and other drops may offset that. Measure full routes before claiming depletion. Track charm/lure stock at each step and long unlucky runs, not just average success probability.

## Proposed acquisition landmarks

1. Owned weak starter at avatar selection; no catches needed to enter an ordinary playable deck.
2. Within the first three checked successes, one direct choice of a simple common spell/creature fitting the starter. Include a generic option; respect current collection eligibility.
3. Around five new successes, enough proposed coins for the first pack. Show compatibility/quota changes in the deck workshop.
4. Each chapter gives at least one direct strategy choice and one free pack proposal, plus existing catches/tactic rewards. Introduce changing-rule cards before complicated scheduled/repeatable events.
5. Late optional challenges provide unusual strategies and rarities without making the taught path require lucky pulls.

These are pacing targets for #124, not new rewards currently configured. Record time and decisions needed to make a useful deck swap, not merely total cards acquired. Unlock generosity must not invalidate backup codes or erase old earned collections.

## Required catching playtest scenarios

Use all avatar types, gentle/hard puzzle settings, first and repeated visits, common through Mythic/Legendary, a lure activated with only one Charm, cancellation/re-entry, failed catches and zero stock. Combine deterministic route simulations with real click/keyboard/touch play. Report probability clarity, choice variety, items spent per successful catch, 90th/99th-percentile failure streaks, lure visits actually used, net supply and a recoverable path after depletion. Do not tune failure out entirely; keep informed choices and make recovery playable.

A future shop must not require selling essential catching stock to buy cards. Pack and catch acquisitions must explain their different instance/foil rules. #125 remains open until actual evidence and any tested fixes are delivered.

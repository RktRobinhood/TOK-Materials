All six batches: seventy-two creature mechanics, twelve per colour. This is a creative cast/brief pass, not new game definitions or commissioned images. Mechanical IDs/costs/rarities/acquisition remain stable; effects follow the authoritative catalogue, with explicit proposal revisions such as #144 recorded in design/reviews/. Shipping names/silhouettes are builder selections. Public-role/source links support the reference, not the invented joke or a measured claim of familiarity among Danish sixteen-year-olds. Public references were checked on 12 October 2026; thin or inaccessible pages are flagged for builder verification before art; the satire concerns public work and uses original text.

Follow ART-BIBLE.md and attach approved style-board plus shipped-creature references before generation. Keep dusk/cute-horror contrast, strong dark outlines and the relevant affinity jewel accent; do not paint a realistic human face on an animal.

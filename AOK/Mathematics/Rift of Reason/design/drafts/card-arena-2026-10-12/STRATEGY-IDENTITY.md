## Two separate synergy conditions

**Construction:** snapshot mono identity from the submitted personal twenty-card deck at battle start. Shared Axioms, draws, board losses and temporary colour changes never recalculate it. Recommend generic cards do not break mono; require one coloured card, preventing six identities for an all-generic deck. Preserve colour quotas/two-copy limits. Casting coloured spells requires no matching live creature.

Resolve **Memory versus Generic** before release: recommend printed Memory counts as the sixth colour despite its “colourless” label; generic helpers/paper tokens are separate. Absent colour never implies Memory. Classification remains proposed.

**Live board:** independently check another friendly creature's different explicit colour at resolution. Recommend current derived colours; generic/tokens do not automatically qualify. These are named abilities, not universal damage modifiers. Use a simple trigger/once-per-turn source cap; avoid duplicate keyword stacks or recursive damage bonuses.

Candidate slots, pending #123 revision:

- **Fable Fawn:** first standout friendly stat swap. Mono-Imagination bonus: swapped ally gains one maximum health/heals one, once per source per turn.
- **Counterexample Crow:** mixed-board Entrance deals one additional damage with a different-colour ally. No extra packet/trigger; normal prevention applies.
- **Generic Small Patch:** mono-deck alternate heals three instead of two. Generic creatures can use the same snapshot for explicitly authored bonuses later; this grants no universal ability or new card.

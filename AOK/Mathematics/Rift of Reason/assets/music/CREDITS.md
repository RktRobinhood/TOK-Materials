# A Little Wonder

Original custom main-menu instrumental generated with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) for Rift of Reason on 11 October 2026.

A gentle plucked-string melody, bell-like notes and warm string pads; a curious fantasy-adventure motif. Generated as a continuous 30-second loop with `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`, then normalized to -22 LUFS. No vocals or borrowed source recording.

`title-theme-source.mp3` preserves the generated take. `title-theme.mp3` is the quieter game asset. `title-theme.json` records the prompt, generation credits, duration, measured levels and file hash. `tools/title-music.mjs` reproduces processing without another generation request.

## The Algorithm's opening soundscape (CC0)

- `server-room.mp3`: edited from [NAS, starting](https://bigsoundbank.com/nas-starting-s1622.html) by **Joseph Sardin**, CC0. Real cooling fans and hard disks; a filtered excerpt with a short overlap at the loop boundary.
- `algorithm-theme.mp3`: [Lost in a bad place (horror ambience loop)](https://opengameart.org/content/lost-in-a-bad-place-horror-ambience-loop) by **congusbongus**, CC0. Dark string pads and distant beacon-like tones, normalized quietly beneath dialogue. This existing soundscape introduces the recurring villain atmosphere; it is not a newly composed exclusive song.

Both sources are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original recordings, source/download URLs, hashes, exact processing and measured levels are retained in `design/source-audio/cold-open-65/`. `tools/cold-open-audio.mjs` reproduces the local processing. No ElevenLabs request or credit spend was used for this revision.

03 / CROSS-REPORT SIGNAL

Fingerprint proximity

Only sufficiently long open responses are compared. Low Hamming distance means the response fingerprints are unusually similar; it does not explain why. Shared teaching, common prompts, collaboration, quotations, or copying can all increase similarity.

INTERPRETATION RULE

Signals trigger questions, not conclusions.

Use a flag to decide what to inspect in the student PDF: compare the first and final answer, check whether revisions respond to the activity, see whether source choices appear in the final reasoning, and talk to the student if the process and product do not cohere.

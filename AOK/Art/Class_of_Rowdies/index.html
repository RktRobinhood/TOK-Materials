Giovanni Battista Piranesi / Le Carceri d'Invenzione / 1761 / public domain
You chose: the room

Architecture matters.

Piranesi makes the person tiny inside the structure. That is not a bad metaphor for school: rules, assessment, teacher behavior, peer judgment and routines can make agency easier or harder.

Self-determination theory treats autonomy as one of the psychological needs relevant to motivation. In classrooms, autonomy-supportive conditions can support more voluntary engagement.

This path is not an excuse for the teacher. If the room punishes voice, the room has to change.

Vincent van Gogh / Prisoners Exercising / 1890 / public domain
You chose: the role

A routine can become a costume.

Van Gogh's prisoners move constantly and still go nowhere. The unsettling idea is that activity can coexist with passivity: arrive, comply, wait, complain, leave, repeat.

Psychology uses the term learned helplessness for a pattern in which repeated lack of control can reduce later attempts to use control that has become available.

This is an analogy, not a diagnosis of a class. Its useful question is simpler: when circumstances change, do our habits notice?

A better room can create possibility. It cannot speak through you.

The film works because both sides move. The teacher creates conditions. The students eventually fill them.

You chose: the feed

The easiest next move is still a move.

APA's adolescent social-media advisory is deliberately more nuanced than “phones are bad.” It says online experience is shaped both by what young people choose and by platform features, including recommended content and endless scrolling.

The autonomy question is not whether technology controls you. It is whether you notice the moment a designed default starts impersonating your intention.

Original visual for this page: the gold bars are deliberately attractive. A constraint does not have to feel unpleasant to reduce choice.

James Ensor / The Intrigue / 1890 / public-domain work via Wikimedia Commons
You chose: the crowd

A mask can be chosen and still become a cage.

Ensor's crowd is comic, ugly, theatrical and difficult to read. Everyone has a face, but not necessarily their own face.

Social comparison is not automatically harmful, and social media can support real connection. But when approval becomes the steering mechanism, somebody else starts writing the criteria for a successful life.

If everyone applauds the same destination, how many people actually chose it?

Influence is real. Destiny is a stronger claim.

Agency is not pretending that algorithms, friends, teachers, money or expectations do not influence you. It is noticing influence early enough to decide whether it gets the final word.

Fork 03 / intention versus action

What happens when you do not feel like it?

Pride is cheap if it only exists when motivation is high. Choose which problem you want to confront.

This choice is about self-direction, not obedience.
Francisco Goya / The Sleep of Reason Produces Monsters / 1799 / public domain
You chose: wait

Motivation makes a terrible gatekeeper.

Goya's image was not made as a productivity poster. Here it works as a provocation: what takes over while the directing mind is absent?

There is a well-studied gap between intending to do something and actually doing it. Waiting to feel ready can turn mood into the person with the key.

No hustle-culture claim is being made here. Rest can be chosen. So can delay. The question is whether either is deliberate.

You chose: make a rule

Discipline can be autonomy with a memory.

Psychologists call one version an implementation intention: an if-then plan that links a situation to a chosen response.

“If discussion opens, then I ask one real question.” “If I disagree, then I say why.” The point is not that the rule is imposed. The point is that you wrote it before the easier option arrived.

Research has repeatedly found that these specific plans can improve follow-through compared with a goal intention alone.

Discipline is not the opposite of freedom.

Obedience follows somebody else's direction. Self-discipline can be the structure you build so that a choice you actually value survives boredom, embarrassment and convenience.

The prisoners begin actually chained.

That matters. The allegory is not “people are trapped because they are lazy.” Their first reality is genuinely constrained. The interesting question comes when another direction becomes possible.

09 / autonomy has a cost

No map is coming.

Once you stop outsourcing every next move, uncertainty arrives. That is not a defect in autonomy. It is part of the price.

You do not control the whole landscape. You do control whether you remain only a spectator inside it.

Caspar David Friedrich / Wanderer above the Sea of Fog / c. 1817 / public domain
back to the film / back to this room

A teacher can create space. A teacher cannot occupy your place in it.

The class in the film changes when students stop being a problem to be managed and start becoming authors of what happens.

That does not make the teacher irrelevant. It makes responsibility shared.

You are allowed to dislike an activity. You are allowed to ask for something different. You are allowed to disagree. You are allowed to bring something in. You are allowed to make the room stranger, smarter, funnier, more difficult or more yours.

But if you use none of that space, passivity is no longer only something the room did to you.

You are too capable to rehearse powerlessness in a room where you have options.
last fork / no correct-looking button

Does class happen to you or with you?

Circumstances write some lines. Teachers write some lines. Other people write some lines. The question is whether you insist on writing any of the next ones.

that is still a choice

Then own it.

No punishment follows. No door slams. But there is a difference between “nothing can be done” and “I am choosing not to do anything with the options I have.”

The second statement is harsher. It is also more honest - and honesty preserves the possibility of changing your mind later.

not a promise to be “good”

Author something specific.

Shared authorship means the teacher changes things too. So write both sides of the bargain.

Sources, art, psychology and technical notes

Film
Short Film - Class of Rowdies, YouTube video supplied for this activity: open on YouTube. Short phrases on the page are taken from the transcript supplied with the project.

YouTube watch gate
The screening uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embed, hides native seeking controls, tracks normal forward playback and reverses obvious jumps. The film expands to a viewport-filling VHS-style screening. If YouTube itself requires sign-in, the original-video link and the 30-second leaking-glass exit prevent a dead end. This remains a soft classroom gate rather than tamper-proof enforcement.

Self-determination theory
Ryan and Deci's framework describes autonomy, competence and relatedness as basic psychological needs relevant to motivation. Classroom autonomy support is an established application of the theory.

Learned helplessness
Used here only as a conceptual analogy: repeated uncontrollability can reduce later attempts to act even when control becomes available. This page is not diagnosing students.

Social media
APA's 2023 advisory states that adolescent social-media experience is shaped by both user choices and platform features, and specifically discusses features such as recommended content and endless scrolling. It also emphasizes that social media is not inherently harmful or beneficial.

Implementation intentions
Gollwitzer and colleagues describe if-then plans as a self-regulation strategy linking a future cue with a chosen response; research reviews report improved goal follow-through in many contexts.

Public-domain art
Piranesi, Van Gogh, Francisco Goya, Jan Saenredam, Caspar David Friedrich and James Ensor are used as interpretive prompts. Where a museum image cannot load, the site falls back to a local graphic interpretation. Plato uses a purpose-built cave map and links the historical Saenredam engraving. These classroom readings are not claims about the artists' intended meanings.
